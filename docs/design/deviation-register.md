# Deviation Register

Every intentional difference between what was shipped and what the client reviewed (the mockups `image5` Inicio and `image12` Quiénes somos in `docs/client-proposal/mockups/`, and the client proposal). Nothing here is an accident: each row says what changed, why, which mockup it departs from, and what would let us undo or revisit it.

Sources: `openspec/changes/platform-foundation/exploration.md` section 5.8 (initial register), `openspec/changes/design-system-and-public-site/proposal.md` (baseline deviations A1-A9), `design.md` (DS7, DS12, OQ1, OQ2, finding 7) and the evidence recorded in `tasks.md` for slices F0-F7.

Status values: **Active** (in the shipped site), **Known issue** (a defect we have not fixed, with the reason).
